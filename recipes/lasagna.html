Lasagna

Description

Imagine a tower of pure comfort, fresh from the owen and bubbling with life.
This easy lasagna is a warm, bubbling tower of pure comfort that comes together with just 10 minutes of prep time. It layers tender pasta ribbons with a savory, slow-simmered ground beef meat sauce and a velvety, parsley-flecked cottage cheese filling. Every layer is packed with three rich cups of gooey mozzarella and crowned with a crisp, golden Parmesan crust for a crowd-pleasing dinner that satisfies everyone at the table.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 jar spaghetti sauce
  • 32 ounces cottage cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozarella cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 teaspoons dried parsley
  • salt
  • ground black pepper
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • ½ cup water

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients and preheat the oven to 350 degrees F(175 degrees C).
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook and stir ground beef in a hot skillet until browned and crumbly, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain and discard grease. Stir in spaghetti sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Combine cottage cheese, 2 cups of mozarella cheese, eggs, ½ of the grated Parmesan cheese, dried parsley, salt, and pepper to taste in a large bowl.
  4. Spread ¾ cup of sauce in a 9x13-inch baking dish. COver with 3 uncooked lasagna noodles, 1 ¾ cups of cheese mixture, and ¼ cup sauce; repeat layers once more. Top with remaining 3 noodles, sauce, mozarella, and Parmesan cheese. Pour ½ cup water along the edges of the dish. Cover tightly with aluminium foil.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes. Uncover and bake for an additional 10 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
